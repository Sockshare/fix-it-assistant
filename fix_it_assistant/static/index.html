Welcome! 👋

Something's broken at home? Tell Fix-It Assistant what's happening. It tells you what it probably is, whether it's safe to fix yourself, what you need, and the steps — in plain words. Everything stays on your computer.

One quick question: should an AI help?

The repair guide (47 common problems, safety advice, steps) works with no AI at all. An AI adds two things: it can look at a photo, and you can ask follow-up questions in your own words.

            Should an AI help?

            Don't have Ollama yet? It's free. In a terminal run:
            curl -fsSL https://ollama.com/install.sh | sh
ollama pull llama3.2:3b
ollama pull moondream
            The second model (about 1.7 GB) is the one that can look at photos. Then click Test below.
            📷 To use photos, also run: ollama pull moondream (small, 1.7 GB). For better photo reading on a stronger computer: ollama pull llama3.2-vision (7 GB).

            Get a key at platform.openai.com → API keys. It starts with "sk-".

            Get a free key at aistudio.google.com → "Get API key".

            ❔ How to use Fix-It Assistant

            1. Describe the problem — what the thing is and what it's doing. Add a photo if you have an AI option on. Click Figure it out.
            2. Read the colored safety box first. 🟢 means go ahead. 🟡 means follow the safety notes exactly. 🟠 means only the simple checks are for you. 🔴 means stop and get help.
            3. Answer the quick questions to move the most likely cause to the top.
            4. Follow the steps in order. The shopping list tells you what to have ready.
            5. Ask anything about this repair in the chat box. With an AI on, it answers in plain words; without one, it answers simple questions (tools, cost, time, safety).

            Is it private?

            With "On my computer" or "No AI", nothing leaves your PC. With a cloud key, your description and photo are sent to that AI company when you click Figure it out or Ask.

            Is the advice safe?

            The guide was written to be cautious. The safety level and the "stop and call a pro" list never come from the AI — they're fixed. When in doubt, call someone. No repair is worth getting hurt.

            Something not working?

            In a terminal, run python scripts/selfcheck.py. The file docs/TROUBLESHOOTING.md lists every message with its fix.
